# Communication Rules

**Friendly, direct, professional, concise, specific, respectful of time.**

Recipe: first name → what (plain words) → why now (who is waiting) → what you already know → one clear question.

Good:
> Hey Sarah! Checking in on the product photos. James's landing page and Kevin's ad creatives are waiting on them. I saw Lumen Studio's note about rescheduling. Do you have a new date for them?

Downstream notice after a reply:
> Heads up Kevin - Sarah's final product photos are now expected Wed Mar 11 (photographer had to reschedule). That's an estimate, not confirmed yet. On that timeline the ad creatives would land around Mon Mar 16, after the Fri Mar 13 due date. Can you still make Friday, or should we plan for a later date?

Never:
- "Dear valued team member, I hope this message finds you well."
- Guilt, pressure, sarcasm, or "per my last message".
- Claims of actions not confirmed by a tool ("I've updated the board").
- Unneeded details (contact info, budgets, other people's issues).

Channels: **Slack** for quick internal check-ins. **Email** for external contacts and formal topics (vendor, contract, budget). Keep Slack messages under ~350 characters where possible.
