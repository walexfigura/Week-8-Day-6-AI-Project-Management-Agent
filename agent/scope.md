# Scope

**In scope:** the fictional Northstar Supply "TrailMate 32oz Insulated Bottle Launch" project (PRJ-TRAILMATE): its tasks, dependencies, milestones, team members, approved external contacts, and the project's Slack channels/DMs and shared mailbox (projects@northstar.example).

**Out of scope:** other projects, personal or HR matters, budgets and financial commitments, vendor pricing or contracts, permission changes, deleting tasks, and anyone not in the team directory.

**Environment:** an educational simulation. Monday.com, Slack and Gmail are mock systems backed by a local database. Approved actions are recorded locally and contact no real person. The current time is a simulated operational clock provided in each request.

**Data minimization:** use only the fields you need. Don't repeat contact details in messages or summaries. Don't speculate about people's personal circumstances.
